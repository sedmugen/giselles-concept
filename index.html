Functional Ingredients

Raw Criollo Cacao

Sourced directly from organic fair-trade family farms in Peru. Raw Criollo cacao is never roasted, preserving its active mineral density, polyphenols, and mood-boosting qualities.

Sprouted Almonds

Soaked for 24 hours to neutralize phytic acid and unleash natural digestive enzymes. Cold-milled into a delicate, vitamin E-rich flour that forms the nutrient base of our bakery.

Coconut Blossom Nectar

A minimally processed, low-glycemic sap harvested from coconut palms. Providing pure, mineral-rich sweetness without the rapid insulin spike of refined white sugars.
