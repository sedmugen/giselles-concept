Ingredients & Allergies

Our bars are made with organic peanut butter, sprouted almonds, plant-based protein, organic dates, single-origin cacao, and coconut blossom nectar. Free from gluten, soy, corn, preservatives, dairy, and eggs. Contains peanuts.

Delivery & Shipping

We ship nationwide using specialized cooling insulation packaging to keep your treats fresh. Inside & Outside CA customers can choose delivery dates at checkout. Orders placed after 11am PST ship the following day. Monday through Friday shipping.

Storage & Shelf Life

This product must be refrigerated. Our protein bars maintain peak freshness for up to 4 weeks in the refrigerator, and can be frozen for 6 to 7 months for extended enjoyment.
